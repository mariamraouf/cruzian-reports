Why this zero differs from last week's

Last week's zero was arithmetic — seven clicks will not produce an enquiry. This week's is a real question. But until 17 November, nine of the ten phone links on the site fired nothing into Google Ads, including every link on the Personal Services page, where almost all of this week's spend landed. If anyone called, there is no record of it. That is fixed and verified on the live site.
